# Movie-readable wreck polish — P2 build plan

**Player outcome.** From the moving chase camera, a front hit folds the nose, a side hit tears a door area, and a rear hit crushes the tail. One or two painted panels peel away; small metal and glass fragments, sparks, the crash sound and a short camera hold show where the energy went. The wreck then settles without leaving a mound, and retry starts pristine. This is the traffic-car [P2 roadmap slice](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/179); new player-model damage across the garage remains P3 scope.

## Existing seams and scope

The six-kind [traffic catalogue](../../src/world/carModels.ts) already renders persistent front, rear, left and right crush through shared instanced geometry. [Traffic](../../src/world/traffic.ts) owns encounter IDs, newly wrecked cars and crushed collision shapes; that matched hull stays authoritative. The existing [impact severity](../../src/core/impactSeverity.ts) provides estimated normal closing speed in m/s when Jolt has no solved impulse. [Crash audio](crash-audio.md) supplies impact, metal, glass and grind choices, while [Impact Time](impact-time-aftertouch-plan.md) and the takedown camera own the short focus. Current [breakable props](../../src/world/breakableProps.ts) already use pooled physical fragments; car trim must have a separate, smaller visual pool so a pile of fragments cannot launch a vehicle.

One post-step wreck presentation record carries encounter ID, model kind, affected zone, contact point, relative normal and tangential speed, severity, and the new crush amount. Traffic/contact code creates it once per meaningful change; rendering and audio consume it after the physics step. Deduplicate by encounter and zone, never by recycled Jolt body ID. A repeated scrape may sustain sparks and grind, but must not detach the same panel again or replay a hard bang each step. The +Z catalogue nose versus travel-axis conversion remains in one place; front, rear and side effects must follow the same transform as the crushed mesh and hull.

P0C crushed traffic shapes, A0 catalogue/material contracts and the C2 junction are integration dependencies. The first PR can be driven on the takedown road before C2 merges; the second consumes C2's stable wreck event. One integrator owns the traffic/main contact record while E3 Face Off reads that record for race credit. In scope are the six traffic kinds and their near, physical wrecks. Out are soft bodies, collidable trim, detailed destruction on 4 px cars, new player-class damage, changes to crash scoring, and a second camera or audio authority.

## Budget and CTO drive gates

Start with **two panels**, then raise to the four-active global cap only after the first burst gate passes. Initial simultaneous limits are **48 spark streaks, 24 metal flecks and 16 glass pieces**, with at most **three added instanced effect draws**; these are proposed ceilings to measure, not a quality target. Sparks last at most 0.8 s; small fragments at most 2 s; panels settle or fade by 3 s. Pool at boot, use no particle shadows or full-screen transparent sheets, and spawn no effect or Jolt body for a purely visual wreck beyond **400 m**. Preserve the existing keep-radius policy and cap awake non-player bodies outside it at **96**.

The CTO drives the **same** traffic kind from front, side and rear at **25–35 m/s normal closing speed**, then a low-speed glancing scrape, all from a moving chase view without debug marks. Two viewers should name the struck zone from the bent body before seeing the detached part; the part must come from that zone, match paint, clear the wheels, and not snag a second hit. A scrape must grow its mark and make grinding sparks/audio without glass or a crash explosion. Repeat on a bus or box truck, a four-to-five-car junction pileup, a knocked car into a wall, a settled wreck re-hit, and retry/rejoin; no floating shards, ghost collision, repeated detach, stale audio or leftover effects. The approved crushed hull and traffic queue behavior must still pass.

On the Intel UHD 620 Vulkan box, use one headed **1280×720 vsync-off** browser at a time and interleave main/PR/main/PR on the same route, with matched visible-car counts and no competing CPU gate. Outside the burst require median fps **at least 0.95× main and at least 20 fps**, and full-step p99 **at most 1.10× main**. For the **two seconds around a four-to-five-car impact**, require full-step p99 **at most 12 ms**; report lowest fps, frame/Jolt/full-step p99, awake non-player bodies, draw calls, active panels/particles and audio voices rather than treating deliberate slow motion as a speedup. After ten pileups and retries, active counts and memory return to baseline. The CTO makes the final moving-view call on the target laptop.

The main risks are duplicated panel geometry, an axis reversal, burst fill cost, an effect following a recycled body, and a debris mound. First prove one near sedan hit with two non-colliding panels against the known mound route; only then add the six-kind anchors and material burst. If the UHD 620 gate misses, shorten lifetime and reduce pool occupancy before adding more geometry or physics.
