# One Crash-mode junction (C2) — build plan

**Player outcome.** Choose **Crash Junction**, pick one of two launch approaches, and drive into moving city traffic at 25–45 m/s. The first wreck enters C1 Impact Time: steer the wreck with Aftertouch, choose a visible ×2 pickup or avoid a harmful pickup, earn one **CRASHBREAKER** after four distinct vehicle wrecks, then see a counted **DAMAGE** total and bronze/silver/gold result. Enter resets the same traffic pattern immediately so a better line can beat the locally saved best. This is the single-junction [roadmap C2 slice](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/179), not a venue pack.

## Event contract and dependencies

Use the east city junction reserved by [W1's open city work](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/208): x = +275 m, z = 0, with a 24 m arterial, 20 m cross street and eight shunt walls outside the clear throat. Offer a south arterial launch and a west cross-street launch, each signed before a short countdown, with at least 90 m sight to the first unavoidable car. Give this event its own seeded 12-car crossing schedule and reserve the existing [`MAX_DRIVING = 12`](../../src/world/traffic.ts) physical slots for those cars during the run. Normal city traffic density and race boost rules stay unchanged. No car may spawn inside the player, a wall or a wreck; cars already in the junction keep their collision bodies through the pileup.

The [C1 plan](impact-time-aftertouch-plan.md) owns bounded wreck steering, slow motion and camera priority. C2 consumes that episode and sends it to a result instead of the usual nearest-road respawn. The [Road Rage event shell](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/201) and [result/best work](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/203) are pending, as is the W1 junction; merge their interfaces before C2 integration. One developer owns `src/main.ts`, contact attribution, map selection and event results during each merge.

Score **actual new damage**, not every contact callback: one fixed vehicle value per distinct wrecked encounter ID (proposed hatch/sedan 500, van/pickup 750, box truck/bus 1,000), plus one small value per uniquely broken prop. The existing [`CrashScore`](../../src/core/crashScore.ts) is a free-drive prop chain and should remain separate. A C2 ledger records encounter and prop IDs, cause, model kind, base award and pickup state; pooled body IDs never become score identities. A ×2 pickup doubles the subtotal once; a visible harmful pickup halves it once, never below zero. Both belong only to Crash mode and respond to the player or Aftertouch wreck, not passing AI. Start medals at **2,000 / 4,000 / 8,000** damage points, then freeze values, targets and seed in a rules version before approval. Result follows two seconds without new damage, with a 15-second wall-time cap after the first player wreck; no late award crosses the result boundary.

## CTO drive gates, budget and risks

Drive **both approaches at 25, 35 and 45 m/s** from a fresh start. A small timing or lane change should produce a different visible chain and total. Steer the wreck into traffic, try both pickups, earn READY from four cars (props and the player do not count), fire exactly one Crashbreaker, and see blast victims add once. Retry in **≤2 s** to the identical seed, then reload and see the best preserved. With the same fixed-step input and seed, require the same victim IDs, awards and result **10/10** times; a new line must be able to improve the score. No pickup, score or blast may leak into ordinary racing or Road Rage.

On the UHD 620, use one headed **1280×720 vsync-off** browser at a time and interleave main/PR/main/PR on the same fixed circuit route. Require median fps **≥0.95× main** and full-step p99 **≤1.10× main** outside the crash burst, plus **≥20 fps** on the Crash junction outside it. In a two-second 12-car burst require full-step p99 **≤12 ms** and **≤96 awake non-player bodies outside the 30 m keep radius**; report lowest fps, Jolt/full-step p99, 4/8 px visible-car counts and the renderer. Reuse crash audio and geometry; cap the blast at one instanced draw with 48 GPU-only shards and no new rigid debris. These box numbers are relative evidence; the CTO makes the final moving-view call on his own machine.

The first cheap probe is 12 existing physical traffic cars at the junction **without** pickups or explosion. A pileup can exhaust the body pool, double-count pooled IDs, stall the route or make the blast look like bouncing toys. Add the ledger before effects, preserve wreck collision shapes, cap upward blast motion, and profile the awake-body tail after the cars settle. Keep W1 geometry, C1 wreck control and E1 results as explicit merge dependencies rather than rebuilding them inside C2.
