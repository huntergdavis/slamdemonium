# Sense of speed (S1) — build plan

**Player outcome.** With the optional lab HUD hidden, the player can distinguish **30, 60 and boosted ~85 m/s** by the road, camera and sound; recognize boost onset, a clean near miss and a heavy hit without hunting for a number; and still see the next hazard. This implements [roadmap S1](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/179) after R1 and the crash-response seam. It tunes existing systems instead of adding another camera, impact estimator or persistent HUD card.

## Decisions and budget

- **Camera/world:** Keep the current [speed-follow chase rig](../../src/render/cameraRig.ts) and its **115° vertical FOV guard**. Tune `fovSpeedGain`, `fovBoostKick`, `camSpeedPull`, `camSpeedClose` and `camShake` separately; a wider FOV can make the hero car and traffic look smaller. Keep the road/shoulder and oncoming lane visible through loops, the chicane and takedown walls. No new roadside physics props for an artificial flow effect. [Prior A/B reasoning](speed-and-vibe.md#camera-finding-the-existing-fov-curve-needs-a-final-bound)
- **Speed lines and kick:** Retune the existing [32-spoke peripheral canvas](../../src/render/speedCues.ts), which already protects the central **64%** of the view; at rest it should disappear. Use the one [shared impact severity](../../src/core/impactSeverity.ts) for a short heavy-hit camera impulse, with a bounded decay and per-contact guard so a wall grind does not shake every physics step. Ordinary light knocks stay readable. A boost onset should expand the view smoothly, not jump one frame.
- **Sound:** Keep the existing AudioWorklet engine, three tyre layers, boost sustain/attack, master mute and lazy gesture unlock. Blend a low wind/road-noise component into the worklet's existing engine output, so no sixth continuous voice or downloaded loop is necessary; tune engine load against it at 30/60/85 m/s. Drive one short left/right whoosh from the [once-per-pass near-miss detector](../../src/core/trafficEvents.ts), which already voids a pass after contact. Extend its event record with pass side/closing speed only if needed; schedule audio after the physics step, never inside the detector. Give crash/takedown transients priority over the whoosh. Keep the existing **15 total voice** cap and the crash-audio mix from [PR #200](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/200). Browser sound still starts only after a real gesture; mute, pause and resume must never replay a stale whoosh. [Audio contract](../../src/audio/README.md#timing-surfaces-and-voices) [Chrome autoplay policy](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/web-audio-autoplay)
- **Quiet HUD:** Reuse the [single short traffic-event label](../../src/ui/hud.ts) and current speed/boost/map instruments. With HUD off at rest, S1 adds **zero** persistent cards; near miss and heavy hit may show one brief readable cue. Road Rage's timer/target and takedown counter retain their mode-specific priority.

**Motion blur is a measured option, not a default promise.** First record no-blur and blur-on runs with the same camera/lines/audio. A world-only, single-composite prototype must leave the HUD, car nose, nearby lane edges and hazards sharp enough to steer. A pair of 8-bit RGBA color targets alone is about **7 MiB at 1280×720 DPR1**, **40 MiB at 1440×900 DPR2**, before depth/MSAA; extra sampling costs GPU time too. True object-motion blur would need more scene data. Keep blur disabled on mobile and by default unless the CTO prefers it _and_ the hardware gate passes. A rejected prototype ends S1 with speed lines/camera/audio, no postprocess. [Three.js postprocessing](https://threejs.org/manual/pages/post-processing.html) [render-target contract](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/WebGLRenderTarget.html)

## Browser gate and risks

For each PR, run the same 45-second circuit drive on **same-day main / PR / main / PR**, headed 1280×720 Intel UHD 620, vsync off, one GPU browser at a time. Hold route, camera preset, seed, render scale and visible 4/8 px traffic counts equal. Require median fps **≥0.95× main**, full-step p99 **≤1.10× main**, no slower simulation advance and R1's **≥20 fps** floor; report frame p99 and renderer too. A blur candidate additionally needs a within-build off/on pair at 720p and at **1440×900 DPR2**: no detectable frame-tail regression beyond each variant's repeat spread, no unreadable hazard, and acceptable memory on mobile. If a positive blur result depends on a quieter host or different traffic count, discard it. [Browser interleave rule](../PERFORMANCE.md#reading-a-gate-number-honestly-2026-10-04)

The CTO's final drive is the actual gate: normal circuit and takedown map, HUD off, 30/60/85 m/s, one near miss, one boost onset and one ≥20 m/s hit. They should call out each cue and steer through the next hazard without a lingering blur, camera look-away or new card. Record gesture-to-audio state, mute, voice count/steals, clip peak, FOV trace, kick duration, fps and full-step p99.

**Risks / cheapest probes.** FOV and camera distance can fight each other: A/B them one at a time before changing defaults. A repeated contact can make an uncontrolled shake: replay one wall grind before opening traffic. Wind can mask the new metal mix: audition a matched-speed quiet bed and crash after #200 lands, with the same limiter path. A near-miss whoosh may fire late or on the wrong side: trace the detector's closest-point/side on two natural passes and one touching pass. Blur may erase lane information or cost too much at DPR2: one isolated world-only pass decides it before any renderer refactor. S1 owns camera/audio/HUD integration; A1 owns the car visual, and `main.ts` merges serially.
