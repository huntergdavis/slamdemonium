# Ship-speed and fidelity lock (R2) — build plan

**Player outcome.** Drive a full city, coast, highway and takedown tour with dense moving traffic, the chosen class car and readable wrecks. Road speed stays tied to real time, a pileup does not freeze the chase view, and cars do not visibly change shape at an LOD boundary. The CTO can finish a 10 km circuit lap and one event of every tour format on his **8 GB M1** without sacrificing the approved traffic or crash payoff. This is [roadmap R2](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/179), the final content and performance lock after the venue, car, event and wreck slices merge.

## Scope and measurement contract

**In:** profile the full built content; fix the measured render/physics costs; make simulation pace, frame tails and LOD transitions observable; finish a route-by-route HUD, camera and audio A/B. Preserve the approved density, car handling, collision/score rules, image resolution and crash effects. **Out:** hiding traffic to raise fps, shrinking the 1280×720 gate, lowering physics correctness, replacing Jolt or shipping an unproven WebGPU renderer. P3 player damage enters this lock only if the CTO approves it.

The known debts have different signatures. At low fps, [`FixedStepLoop`](../../src/core/loop.ts) caps catch-up steps and clamps frame gaps; a 60-second wall drive can advance less than 60 seconds of game physics even when `droppedSeconds` looks small. A pileup can put a long tail in frame time even if median fps is steady. A traffic or hero LOD switch can pop despite a good frame number. Record **wall elapsed, `totalSteps/physicsHz`, `droppedSeconds`, frame p50/p99 and worst frame, full-step/Jolt p50/p99, renderer, 4/8 px in-frame cars, active/awake bodies, draws, textures and transferred asset bytes** on every candidate. `timeScale` must be 1 for the pace gate; pause and intentional Impact Time are measured separately. Reuse the [headed, vsync-off, interleaved browser method](../PERFORMANCE.md#reading-a-gate-number-honestly-2026-10-04), one GPU run at a time. A within-main thermal spread wider than a candidate delta means **no detectable change**, not a speedup.

Before choosing an optimization, repeat R1's same-scene toggles for shadows, traffic visuals and resolution at 1280×720 and 320×180. Keep camera, seed, class, density and visible-car counts fixed. If the cost scales with pixels, work on fill/overdraw; if it remains at 320×180, inspect CPU updates and Jolt; if it appears only during an impact, profile the two-second contact/effect window. Do not land a visual tradeoff without a moving chase A/B at 35 and 85 m/s.

## Final acceptance matrix

On the **Intel UHD 620 at 1280×720, vsync off**, require **≥30 fps median on each city, coast and highway venue**, with takedown also held to that tour target; candidate fps **≥0.95× same-day main** and full-step p99 **≤1.10× same-day main** at matched in-frame car counts. Each route keeps median **≥20 cars at 4 px and ≥12 at 8 px**. Run main/PR/main/PR per route after that route exists on both sides; compare a playable parent build during earlier PRs, then repeat against final same-day main. No venue average may conceal a failing route. Include a stationary start, 35 m/s traffic run, 85 m/s boost segment, junction/merge, 4–5-car pileup and 12-car Crash burst; report the named hardware renderer, actual sim/wall advance and the two-second burst separately. If 30 fps fails, leave R2 open and report the limiting subsystem rather than lower the gate.

On the CTO's **8 GB M1**, record browser fps, frame/full-step p99, sim/wall advance and memory on the same-day main and candidate, then require **no measurable regression beyond within-build spread** plus his driving approval. He drives one event of each of seven formats and a complete 10 km circuit lap at 35–85 m/s, including a pileup, then judges speed, class feel, crash payoff, HUD quietness, camera and audio together. Run ten route switches and ten crash/retry cycles without an asset, body, audio-voice or storage leak. Reuse [PERFORMANCE.md's retained JS/WASM checkpoint method](../PERFORMANCE.md) and require at most **10% retained growth** after the initial warm state; report GPU texture counts and peak tab memory separately rather than treating an 8 GB machine as an unlimited budget.

**Dependencies and risk.** Freeze W0–W2 routes, A0/A1–A3 cars, C1/C2/P2 crashes, E1–E4 events and S1 cues before the final lock, but start PR 1 profiling while the seven E4 events are still placeholders. Keep integration through `src/main.ts` serialized. A shadow or LOD optimization may move cost between GPU and CPU or make a hazard disappear; a physics shortcut may make the clock look faster by skipping contacts. Reject both with matched visible density, collision outcomes, simulation advance and moving-view captures. The cheapest first probe is the R1 toggle capture on a full-content city junction, followed by one repeatable pileup before broad tuning.
