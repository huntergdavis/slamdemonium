# City route (W1) — build plan

**Player outcome.** Select a city venue and drive a distinct roughly 3 km loop through two readable intersections. Wide lanes allow passing and shunts; physical crossing traffic and a reserved crash junction add deliberate risks with clear bypasses. Drive either direction. This covers [roadmap W1](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/179); Road Rage and Crash mode can use the venue later.

## Scope and route contract

- Build one **2–4 km** closed loop with two route variants (opposite directions, valid start/checkpoint order). Place both intersections on long sight lines and give each a through lane and a passing bypass. Reserve one junction with two approaches and a future 12-car Crash setup footprint.
- Graybox **24 m arterials** (four roughly 4 m travel lanes plus margins) and **18–20 m cross streets**. These are CTO-drive proposals between today's 16 m circuit and 28 m takedown road. Keep each travel lane **≥3.8 m clear**. At **60 m/s**, expose an unavoidable solid at least **60 m** ahead; aim for **90 m** sight through crossings.
- Author ordinary traffic **≥24 m centre-to-centre** on each stream, targeting **30–45 m** on open boulevards. Crossing cars must follow a second physical path with predictable gaps. Preserve wreck collisions, queueing, safe re-entry and an intact four-rival roster when rivals are enabled. Apply spacing to the city layout, not every map.
- Start with instanced, unlit building blocks. **Out:** an open world, road editor, traffic lights/police, pedestrians, destructible buildings, new circuit signage, jumps, Road Rage rules and Crash scoring.

The existing [road sampler](../../src/world/roadGenerator.ts) and [map contract](../../src/world/maps.ts) can supply the loop and checkpoints. [TrafficSystem](../../src/world/traffic.ts) currently follows one path, so crossing streams need an explicit second-path/record seam. Today's [circuit](../../src/world/circuit.ts) uses a 12 m traffic authoring grid; the [takedown road](../../src/world/takedownCourse.ts) has sparse ordinary cars. No global density slider is required.

## CTO acceptance and risks

Drive **two laps at 35–60 m/s**, one each way. Cross both junctions, pass in another lane, make one wall or traffic takedown, and reach the crash junction without searching for a marker. Cars must fit their lanes; crossing traffic must offer a visible choice, not form an unavoidable wall.

For every PR, compare the shared fixed circuit route with same-day `main` on the **i5-8250U / Intel UHD 620**, headed 1280×720 Chromium, vsync off, one browser at a time. Require median fps **≥0.95× main**, full-step p99 **≤1.10× main**, and no shorter simulation advance. Also drive a fixed city capture on the candidate: require the roadmap **≥20 fps** floor, and compare the same city capture with `main` once the graybox has merged. Report renderer, fps, full-step/Jolt p50/p99, distance, 4/8 px traffic counts and awake bodies during a two-second junction burst. Final W1 density: median **≥20 cars at 4 px**, **≥12 at 8 px**. The CTO's drive decides approval.

**Risks and first probes.** Crossing contacts and queues may raise Jolt cost: begin with one physical crossing and 12 existing cars. Buildings may consume renderer headroom: use instancing without per-building shadows, then run an interleaved hardware A/B. Tight gaps may be unplayable at 60 m/s: drive sparse graybox traffic first, move entries until the bypass reads, then increase density. Keep W1 geometry ownership separate from Road Rage HUD/rules and C2 scoring.
