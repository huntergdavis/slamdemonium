# Grand Prix, Eliminator and a compact World Tour — E4 build plan

**Player outcome.** Open one event map, choose a city, coast or highway challenge, read its objective, drive it, earn a result and medal, and see a new event or car become available. Return later to replay any unlocked event. A three-race Grand Prix carries points between venues; an Eliminator drops the last car after each lap. This is [roadmap E4](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/179), with a small authored tour rather than a long list of duplicated roads.

## Rules and scope

- **Eliminator:** Use a signed **2–3 km closed highway loop** built from W2 road data, with a matching physical deck and ordered gates. Six entrants, including the player, start from the E3 grid. When the leader completes each valid lap, eliminate **one** last surviving entrant by validated gate and station; five cuts decide the winner. Show **SAFE / AT RISK**, the lap and surviving field. Resolve exact ties by stable entrant ID. A missed gate, shortcut, respawn or off-camera rival re-entry cannot create a free lap. An eliminated player gets a frozen result and immediate retry, never control of a ghost car. Proposed medals: survive two cuts / reach the final two / win.
- **Grand Prix:** Run three six-car heats in order: City Sprint, Coast Shoreline and Highway Express. Carry the same named rivals and **points only** between heats; reset damage, boost, traffic wrecks and grid poses before each GO. Proposed points by valid finish place are **10/6/4/2/1/0**; ties use more heat wins, then the final heat's valid finish time. Proposed medals are overall top three / top two / winner; the gold result unlocks one A3 race car. An intermediate result shows the next venue and **Enter: next heat**; after the third heat, show the points table, medal and **Enter: retry GP**. Save no partial GP medal or car reward. Pause and takedown slow motion use simulation time, as in [E3's race contract](race-faceoff-plan.md).
- **Tour:** Start with **one playable event of each of seven formats**—Burning Lap, Road Rage, Crash, Race, Face Off, Eliminator and GP—before filling the catalogue to **at least 18 authored entries** on **at least six selectable routes**. A first version can allocate six city entries (two laps, Road Rage, two Crash approaches, Race), five coast entries (two laps, two Road Rages, Race), and seven highway entries (two laps, two Races, Face Off, Eliminator, GP). The GP card launches its three-venue series. Each card names its venue, route, car eligibility, objective, medal target and explicit prerequisite; locked cards cannot boot through the picker or URL.

Reuse E1/E2's [Road Rage result and best contract](road-rage-plan.md), C2's [Crash ledger](crash-mode-plan.md), E3's race and Face Off reward, and W2's [route identities](coast-highway-plan.md). Keep the existing [`timedRun.ts`](../../src/core/timedRun.ts) for Burning Lap and gate shapes; extend E3's `src/core/raceEvent.ts` for validated multi-lap progress rather than adding a second standings calculation. A3 supplies **at least nine selectable cars**, including **three Crash-only heavyweights**. P3 player damage remains a separate CTO decision. **Out:** 173 events, online accounts/boards, an open world, new wreck physics, and a second rival AI.

## Persistence, CTO gate and budget

Store local progress with an explicit schema version. A result identity includes **event ID, route ID, car ID, tuning fingerprint and rules version**; keep best/medal separate from a stable reward ID so a rules update does not silently relock an earned car. Reject corrupt or incompatible records safely; blocked storage keeps a session-only tour. A medal upgrade may unlock the next named card or car, but a later lower result never removes it. Test reload, ten retries, two car choices on one route, changed tuning, and a manually entered locked URL. Before adding all 18 cards, have the CTO finish the seven-format vertical tour in one browser session and identify each objective/result without opening telemetry.

On the Intel UHD 620, interleave **main/PR/main/PR** at 1280×720 with vsync off on the same route and comparable rival density; quote a delta only when host load and within-main spread support it. Require median fps **≥0.95× main and ≥20**, full-step p99 **≤1.10× main**, and no lost samples. Separately capture a six-car start, Eliminator cut, GP heat transition and Crash burst; report fps/frame/full-step/Jolt p99, active bodies, 4/8 px cars, renderer and bundle growth. The catalogue is lightweight DOM/data, with no extra always-awake world bodies; target **≤50 KB added gzip** for E4 code and event data and report actual growth. Keep retry-to-control **≤2 s** and save/venue switching free of a full asset reload where possible. The CTO's final gate is seven formats, ≥18 meaningful events, ≥six routes, ≥nine cars, distinct medals/results, one visible new unlock and a clean reload.

**First risks to probe:** a long Eliminator can feel repetitive, so drive the short six-car loop before locking its length; multi-heat GP can carry stale damage or duplicate points, so restart heat two ten times; tour content can become a spreadsheet, so approve the seven-event sequence before authoring eleven more. A URL or old save must never bypass the lock, and a rival crossing a gate during camera slow motion must not reorder the field outside simulation time.
