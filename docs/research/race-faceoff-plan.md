# Circuit Race and a car-winning Face Off — E3 build plan

**Player outcome.** Select **Circuit Race**, start among five rivals, fight through traffic for position, finish a checkpoint-valid lap, and see the order. Then select **Face Off**, beat one named opponent, and choose its newly unlocked car livery on the next drive. This is [roadmap E3](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/179) adapted to the PM's colour/variant reward: a distinct look on the existing player car is the first prize; new handling classes and the full garage remain A2/E4 work.

## Rules and boundaries

- Use the existing **10.1 km circuit**, its start/goal and three checkpoints ([circuit.ts](../../src/world/circuit.ts)). Both events have a stationary six-car or two-car grid, **3, 2, 1, GO**, one lap, live **P1/6** or **P1/2**, the next checkpoint and a finish result. Race and Face Off are separate choices in the current map picker; free drive and Road Rage remain available. A loss offers instant retry, not a reward.
- Give every entrant a stable race identity across near/far body handoffs. Rank by ordered checkpoints, then continuous road station within the current sector; record finish time at the valid goal crossing. A reverse crossing, missed checkpoint, respawn or shortcut cannot increase validated progress. At the player's finish, freeze the result from valid finish times and remaining progress. The event clock is simulation time, paused with the game; takedown slow motion cannot shorten the race.
- Keep contact, takedowns, traffic and earned boost live. The five rivals must drive rather than follow a scripted animation. Race-specific recovery may re-enter a wrecked rival only after a visible hold and penalty, at a safe off-camera point **behind its last valid checkpoint**; it may never gain race position by re-entry. Catch-up is a logged, bounded speed target (initial cap: **+8 m/s** over that rival's ordinary boost-capable target), with physical acceleration and no position teleport. Tune that cap by CTO driving, not a hidden rank-dependent multiplier.
- Face Off uses one identifiable rival and the same course rules. Its win unlocks one **new** high-contrast player livery that matches the opponent; keep #210's existing orange/blue/green choices available, including saved selections. Reuse [#210's car choice](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/210) and model/materials: the prize changes appearance, **not** collider, mass, gearing or speed. Persist a versioned event/route/reward identity in local storage, with a session-only fallback when storage is blocked. A loss, retry or rival-versus-rival wreck never unlocks it.

**In:** grid, start/finish, fair standings, readable race HUD/result, physical rivals, bounded recovery and catch-up, one Face Off and one persistent livery reward. **Out:** GP, Eliminator, a tour map, online boards, new drivetrain classes, a new vehicle mesh and traffic checking. The race can be built on the circuit before W2's extra routes; adding a city Race belongs to E4. Merge Road Rage's [event reset/result work](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/203) and #210 before the final integration, or keep their file ownership serialized.

## Three playable PRs

1. **Six-car race graybox.** The CTO can select Circuit Race, launch beside five distinct rivals, drive a lap and see P1–P6. Add pure `src/core/raceEvent.ts` standings/phase state; author the staggered grid and race variant in `src/world/circuit.ts` and `src/world/maps.ts`; adapt `src/world/traffic.ts` so all five rivals have physical bodies at GO and nearby rivals retain priority within the existing `MAX_DRIVING` pool; wire `src/main.ts`, `src/ui/hud.ts`, `src/ui/ui.css` and the map picker. Reuse [`timedRun.ts`](../../src/core/timedRun.ts) gate geometry, but track each entrant independently. **Drive gate:** two clean GO starts with no overlapping bodies; a deliberate skipped checkpoint and reverse goal give no finish or position gain; a valid lap yields one stable result, and Enter retries to a clean grid in **≤2 s**. Record five rival IDs and separate rival/civilian awake-body counts.
2. **A contestable, legible pack.** The CTO can overtake, be overtaken, shunt a rival and still understand his place. Tune `src/world/rivals.ts` and `src/world/traffic.ts` for race pace/recovery; extend `src/ui/rivalGuidance.ts`, HUD and the read-only `src/core/gameApi.ts` race trace. Keep the nearest one or two labels near the actual cars, not a screen of badges. On physical/visual handoff, carry the solved body pose into the route station before updating standings. **Drive gate:** at least one active rival projects **≥4 px in ≥75%** of normal moving-chase samples (exclude countdown, result and takedown shot); five remain in the roster; a rival can beat a coasting player and a well-driven player can pass it; no visible handoff/recovery pop or sudden standings gain. Fixed-seed replay gives the same winner and valid order. Measure first-grid and pileup p99 separately.
3. **Face Off and the prize.** The CTO can beat the named opponent, see **WIN — LIVERY UNLOCKED**, select that same look in Options, reload and retain it; after a loss the option stays locked. Reuse the race core with a two-car grid; add a small reward store such as `src/core/raceRewards.ts`, extend `src/ui/carChoice.ts`, `src/vehicle/vehicleDefinition.ts` and `src/render/heroCarModel.ts` after #210 lands, and wire result/retry in `src/main.ts` and HUD. **Drive gate:** loss, win, reload, blocked storage and ten retries behave consistently; the opponent and selected prize are recognizably the same livery from chase; the livery leaves measured normal/boost speed within **±2 m/s** of the unmodified hero car.

One integrator owns `main.ts`, event choice, HUD and persistent results across these PRs. Pure standings and livery art can be prepared in parallel, then integrated sequentially. Add focused state tests for gate order, ties and save identity; use real-Jolt and headed browser drives for the grid, re-entry and contact paths.

## CTO approval and performance risk

Drive the circuit at **35–85 m/s**: start mid-grid, overtake and get overtaken, make one takedown, cut a checkpoint on purpose, then finish a valid lap and retry. In Face Off, lose once, win once, select the prize and reload. Reject a numerically correct race if rivals vanish, visibly teleport, queue inertly behind traffic, or the position display disagrees with the cars. Watch a first-corner pileup and a recovered rival from the moving chase view.

Five active rivals may crowd the current **12-body** traffic pool and raise Jolt cost. First prove the six-car graybox grid with normal civilian density; retain traffic visuals while giving nearby rivals physical priority. For each PR, compare interleaved **main/PR/main/PR**, vsync-off **1280×720** on the Intel UHD 620 with the renderer named, same circuit route and civilian density: median fps **≥0.95× main and ≥20**, full-step p99 **≤1.10× main**. Report the extra rival bodies as a content difference rather than claiming identical scenes. Quote a delta only when the within-main spread supports it; log start/contact burst p99 and 4/8 px car counts. The CTO's own machine and moving drive remain the final feel gate.
