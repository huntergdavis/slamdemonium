# Impact Time and Aftertouch — C1 build plan

**Player outcome.** On the takedown road, a player wreck briefly slows the crash. Hold the existing slow-motion control (**T** or **LB + D-pad Down**) and steer the still-physical wreck with **A/D** or the left stick toward another car. A newly wrecked rival can earn one **AFTERTOUCH TAKEDOWN** before the camera returns and the player respawns on the nearest road. The same interaction works in Road Rage after wrecks one and two; its third wreck remains an immediate **FAILED** result. This implements the bounded [roadmap C1 slice](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/179) before Crash mode's separate scoring rules.

## Fit with the current game

[`PlayerDamage`](../../src/core/playerDamage.ts) already identifies the first wreck transition and starts a 1.5 **simulated-second** recovery timer. [`main.ts`](../../src/main.ts) then removes throttle/steer/boost, loses one boost section, and uses `respawnAfterWreck()` for a pristine, stationary nearest-road return. [`TakedownMoment`](../../src/render/takedownMoment.ts) owns a 750 ms wall-time camera beat with 0.4× slow motion; [`Takedowns`](../../src/core/takedowns.ts) credits a newly wrecked rival by encounter ID within a three-second influence chain. Pending [Road Rage M2](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/203) counts each wreck once and blocks respawn after the third.

Give the player wreck **one episode owner** for slow motion, camera and recovery. Start automatically with a short, roughly 0.8 s wall-time Impact Time; holding the existing control extends it, release ends it. Cap the episode at **3 simulated seconds and 6 wall seconds**, then return to live camera/control or nearest-road respawn within **0.5 s**. Do not let the old scaled `PlayerDamage.step(dt)` timer independently expire during that episode: [`FixedStepLoop`](../../src/core/loop.ts) would stretch 1.5 simulated seconds when slowed. The Road Rage clock keeps its existing simulated-time rule; pause, slow motion and render rate must not create score or an extra physics step. Player wreck focus takes priority over, and cancels, any rival `TakedownMoment`; a credited Aftertouch hit keeps the controllable wreck in frame instead of cutting to the victim. Reset both presentation owners on respawn/retry. Keep the existing T toggle outside a wreck; during a wreck read its **held** key/pad state and suppress the toggle edge.

## CTO drive gates and risks

Drive a **25–45 m/s closing-speed** wreck twice from the chase camera: first hands off, then hold T and steer into a second moving rival/traffic car. The two paths must differ visibly without homing. Release T and regain the road/camera within **0.5 s**; a no-input wreck must also recover. Repeat with keyboard and pad, a roof/side landing, a wall, pause/retry, and Road Rage's first, second and terminal third wreck. Confirm **at most one** Aftertouch takedown per wreck, one boost payout, no stale credit after respawn, and no Impact Time for an uncredited AI wreck.

Risks are stacked time scales, a second recovery timer, camera focus fighting steer input, air/righting assists fighting wreck torque, and chain credit leaking through pooled bodies. Prove the earliest real-Jolt two-hit path before camera polish; check a **85 m/s** crash for NaNs, missed CCD contact, floating or snagging. On the UHD 620, run the same-day main/candidate **1280×720 vsync-off** route with one browser at a time: median fps at least **0.95× main**, full-step p99 at most **1.10× main**, and at least **20 fps**. Report a separate two-second crash burst with lowest fps, full-step/Jolt p99 and awake bodies; slowed simulation is not evidence of a rendering speedup. No soft body, player-air steering outside a wreck, Crash-mode pickups, or endlessly prolonged wrecks are in C1.
