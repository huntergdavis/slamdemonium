# Five-class garage (A2) — build plan

**Player outcome.** Open a small garage, compare five unbranded cars by silhouette and plain-language traits, choose **Compact, Muscle, Coupe, Sports or Super**, then drive the same route and immediately feel a different launch, turn, shove and engine voice. The selected class survives retry and reload; the current A1 sports sedan remains the safe default until a player chooses another car. This implements [roadmap A2](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/179), not Crash-mode heavyweights or Face Off rewards.

## Class contract and dependencies

| Class       | Feel to prove in a 30-second blind drive                                                                 | Starting normal / boosted ceiling |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Compact** | Eager launch and tight turn; lightest shove.                                                             | **50 / 68 m/s**                   |
| **Muscle**  | Strong straight-line shove and weight transfer; widest turn.                                             | **54 / 74 m/s**                   |
| **Coupe**   | Balanced launch, drift and recovery between Compact and Sports.                                          | **58 / 78 m/s**                   |
| **Sports**  | Current readable all-round reference with strong corner exit.                                            | **60 / 82 m/s**                   |
| **Super**   | Fastest ceiling and boost, slower low-speed launch than its top-end suggests; needs deliberate steering. | **65 / 85 m/s**                   |

These are **proposed Slamdemonium targets, not Burnout 3 measurements**. Record each class's measured 0–30 m/s time, normal and boosted ceiling, 30° full-lock turn radius at 14 and 28 m/s, mass, wheelbase and impact momentum on one versioned rules sheet. Tune by measured drive, then freeze an approved profile within **±2 m/s** of its ceiling; keep gravity 20 and the existing boost-only top-gear decision. At least **4 of 5** blind class comparisons must be distinguishable after a 30-second drive without reading the label.

The open [A1 import/visual PR](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/210) and [LOD/voice PR](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/211) provide the shared glTF importer, wheel/lamp/damage pivots, far LOD and procedural engine. Reuse one coherent CC0 kit and a shared palette where possible; keep source, version, license and edits in the asset provenance note. A class definition owns a stable ID, display name, visual/LOD paths, four wheel pivots, physical bounds and one immutable tuning/engine profile. The present [vehicle geometry](../../src/vehicle/constants.ts) and [Vehicle](../../src/vehicle/vehicle.ts) assume one 4.8 × 2.16 m car: create the new Jolt shape and mass properties **before** the selected event starts, then match each body's four measured visual/collider edges within **0.3 m**. Do not swap a live car's mass or shape in a contact callback.

The existing [TuningStore](../../src/tuning/store.ts) can supply tyre, assist, steering, mass and speed parameters, but its Grip/Drifty/Raw presets are player tuning choices, not car identity. Apply a class base profile over known defaults, then permitted user adjustments; selecting another class must not inherit the previous car's modifications. The [engine profile](../../src/vehicle/engineProfile.ts) is presentation-only today, so bind the selected class profile into RPM, the one audio worklet and tachometer without pretending gear sound changes physical acceleration. Require **at least three clearly audible engine identities** across five cars; preserve crash ducking, mute and one active voice. [E1's versioned results](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/203) are the dependency for comparable local bests: record class ID and approved tuning version, and migrate the old default best without silently comparing unlike cars.

## CTO drive gates, browser budget and risks

Drive the same circuit and city segment for **30 seconds per class**, blind to the label: launch 0–30 m/s, take a tight chicane, drift, hit the same traffic model at a logged closing speed, boost, and recover from a wall/roof/landing. Name the heaviest and fastest from feel; reject any pair that reads as a recolor. Also drive a ramp and loop in all five cars, check wheel contact and lamp direction from the chase camera, and confirm one-key retry preserves the chosen class while a deliberate new selection changes it. A selection must not change rival or civilian AI, event rules, or the seed.

Keep one selected hero mesh and one worklet voice active in play. Lazy-load full models for the selected class; use cheap garage thumbnails and a shared **512² palette**. Start with **≤12k hero triangles**, **≤8 main-pass draws**, and **≤300 KB compressed transfer for the selected model** from A1; hold the whole five-car optional garage download to **≤1 MiB compressed**, then verify actual built-browser bytes and GPU texture use. On the Intel UHD 620, run headed **1280×720 vsync-off** main/PR/main/PR with matched route, seed and visible traffic: median fps **≥0.95× main**, full-step p99 **≤1.10× main**, and **≥20 fps**. Quote each class's 4/8 px traffic counts and crash-burst Jolt/full-step p99 separately; a faster car is not a faster frame.

The cheapest risk probe is **three placeholder classes before five meshes**. Mass, inertia and tyre changes can break the one-car drift/recovery tune, while a longer nose can snag a loop or make damage detach from wheels. Prove 25–85 m/s wall, traffic, roof, ramp and loop cases with matched bounds before art polish. Keep A2 ownership of class definitions, geometry and garage UI separate from E3's rival/standings files; coordinate the shared main.ts integration after A1 and E1 settle.
