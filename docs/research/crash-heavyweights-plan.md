# Crash heavyweights and garage rewards (A3) — build plan

**Player outcome.** Earn and select a pickup, SUV or bus for Crash Junction. Each starts from the same junction but asks for a different entry line: the pickup can thread a gap, the SUV can turn into a second stream, and the long bus can block a whole lane and grow a pileup. The Garage shows what each vehicle does, how to unlock it, and which choices are Crash-only. This implements [roadmap A3](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/179) on top of the [five-class garage](garage-classes-plan.md) and [Crash Junction](crash-mode-plan.md).

## Scope and vehicle contract

**In:** three unbranded heavyweight silhouettes with matching physical bounds, four grounded wheels, distinct mass/inertia, launch, turning and engine voices; visible locked/unlocked Garage cards; persistent earned rewards; two visual/sound variants among the five race classes and one special reward silhouette, for **at least nine selectable silhouettes overall**. The three heavyweights are eligible only in Crash events. **Out:** licensed vehicles, heavyweights in races, a second junction, new damage scoring, cosmetic loot, and recolors presented as new handling classes. A3 does not change the already approved race gearing or C2 victim values.

Start from the A2 class definition contract: stable vehicle ID, mode eligibility, visual/LOD and voice IDs, wheel pivots, measured collision bounds, mass/inertia and a versioned handling profile. A Crash vehicle is selected **before** the event creates its player Jolt body; never resize a live body or change mass in a contact callback. Reuse the same C2 seed, 12 encounter IDs, scoring ledger, Aftertouch and Crashbreaker across vehicles. The player's own wreck never earns vehicle damage points. A different outcome must come from its geometry and driven line, not a hidden score multiplier or altered victim schedule.

The first tuning sheet should propose, then measure and freeze, normal/boost ceilings around **45/60 m/s pickup, 43/57 SUV, 38/52 bus**. These are Slamdemonium starting targets, **not Burnout 3 measurements**. Record 0–30 m/s launch time, full-lock turn radius at 14 and 28 m/s, mass, wheelbase, collision width/length and crash momentum beside the five A2 cars. The bus must be **at least 2 m longer than the SUV** and still fit both C2 approaches and the 20 m crossing without an invisible blocker. Keep rendered body and collision extremities within **0.3 m** in intact and crushed states. A heavy impact may shove more cars but cannot lift the player's wreck into a persistent mound or pass through a queued vehicle.

**Reward rules.** The A2 sports car remains the playable default. First bronze or better on Crash Junction unlocks pickup, first silver or better unlocks SUV, and first gold unlocks bus; a higher first medal awards all lower tiers at once. Show the requirement and new reward on the result and Garage card. Count a reward once by stable reward ID, independently of score/best and body pool ID; a lower later result never removes it. Reuse E1's versioned local persistence and session-only fallback when storage is blocked. The two race-class visual/sound variants can be earned from Face Off and a named tour medal; E4 Grand Prix gold awards the special race silhouette when that event exists. Do not advertise an unavailable event as an active unlock path before its merge.

## CTO drive gate, budget and risks

From a fresh profile, unlock one heavy car through a real medal and select it without debug hooks. Drive the **same south and west C2 lines at logged 25, 35 and 45 m/s** in the sports car, pickup, SUV and bus; use a second line within each vehicle's turning ability. The CTO should identify which of five blind matched-speed runs used a heavy vehicle from the approach and pileup **at least four times**, see each class make a different viable entry choice, and see a clearly different ledger of distinct wrecked encounter IDs. Require no body/mesh gap over 0.3 m, no spawn overlap, ghost car, wall snag, float or unrecoverable pile. Verify 9 distinct silhouettes overall, three heavy models and their measured normal/boost ceilings, and a bus at least 2 m longer than the SUV. Retry keeps the selected car; selecting a race event rejects Crash-only IDs with a clear Garage explanation.

On Intel UHD 620, compare each staged route with the merged A2+C2 parent build in interleaved **1280×720 vsync-off** runs with matching seed and visible-car counts: median fps **≥0.95× parent and ≥20**, full-step p99 **≤1.10× parent** outside bursts. For each 12-car crash, report two-second minimum fps, Jolt/full-step p99, awake bodies, 4/8 px visible cars and the renderer; keep the [C2 burst gate](crash-mode-plan.md) of full-step p99 **≤12 ms** and at most **96 awake non-player bodies outside 30 m**. Load only the selected full hero mesh and one engine voice during play; use cheap Garage silhouettes/LODs and report added gzip/model/texture bytes. The A2 per-model starting caps are **12k triangles, eight main-pass draws and 300 KB compressed transfer**; verify the bus against actual draw/transfer measurements before raising a cap.

The cheapest risk probe is an untextured **bus collider on the existing C2 junction**. Its length may catch walls, block both crossing streams or overload contact/shape swaps. Validate both approaches and 12-car settling before modeling it. A2 owns the shared vehicle definition and Garage interface; C2 owns junction scoring and event reset; E1/E3/E4 own their reward events. Land those interfaces before A3's final integration and give one developer ownership of `src/main.ts` at a time.
