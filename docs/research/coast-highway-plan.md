# Coast and highway routes (W2) — build plan

**Player outcome.** Choose a coastal drive, a high-speed highway circuit, or the city and recognize each with the lab HUD hidden. The coast asks the player to judge oncoming cars and bends against open water; the highway offers a long, fast sight line interrupted by slower traffic and a signed merge; the city keeps its crossing hazards. Each venue has two meaningful routes. This delivers [roadmap W2](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/179) after the [W1 city stack](https://github.com/huntergdavis/slamdemonium/pull/208), without changing the existing circuit, aquifer or jump placement.

## Route and scope contract

- **Coast, 3–5 km per variant:** Build a point-to-point shoreline road with a headland/inland spur that rejoins it before a shared finish. Select **shoreline** or **headland** as separate checkpoint-valid runs; the spur changes the line and risk, not merely the road color. Give the shore an open sea edge, guardrail and long view; put cliffs and a tighter sequence on the inland line. Aim the coast at **35–65 m/s**. At 60 m/s, a fixed obstacle on the intended line must be visible at least **90 m** before contact, or preceded by a clear warning. Author sparse, mixed-speed two-way traffic with an overtaking gap before the narrow section.
- **Highway, about 10 km:** Reuse the [circuit spine](../../src/world/circuit.ts) in a separate highway map, widening its highway lanes only there. Offer **express** and **interchange** configurations: an authored bypass/rejoin around the first chicane gives a real second path with its own signed gates and safe merge. Preserve the original circuit and its set pieces. A divided fast stream with slower heavy vehicles and a controlled contraflow/merge risk makes this different from the coast. Target **60–85 m/s**, with a **130 m** view of unavoidable fixed hazards at 85 m/s. Do not hide a hard wall just beyond a crest or gantry.
- **City:** Use W1's forward/reverse courses as its two routes; no W2 rewrite of its junction or crossing traffic. Across the three venues, provide **at least six selectable routes** and two meaningful paths or directions per venue. Route names, start/finish/checkpoints, mini-map, retry and saved best must identify the selected variant. A missed gate cannot be repaired by leaving the road.

**Dependencies:** W1's two city routes and crossing-path pattern, R1's render budget, and E1's route/checkpoint/result format. **Out:** open world, general road editor, new race or Crash rules, aquifer relocation, half-pipe or ramp placement, and per-object shadow-casting scenery. Coast and highway get separate traffic mixes and silhouettes using pooled/instanced assets, not extra always-awake physics props. The [map contract](../../src/world/maps.ts), [road sampler](../../src/world/roadGenerator.ts), [traffic system](../../src/world/traffic.ts) and [map choice](../../src/world/mapChoice.ts) are the seams. Do not change `src/` in this research PR.

## Browser budget, CTO gate and risks

At each PR, run the same fixed circuit drive on **same-day main / PR / main / PR**, one headed browser at a time on the **i5-8250U / Intel UHD 620**, 1280×720 with vsync off, as [PERFORMANCE.md](../PERFORMANCE.md#reading-a-gate-number-honestly-2026-10-04) requires. Require median fps **≥0.95× main**, full-step p99 **≤1.10× main**, no slower simulation advance and **≥20 fps**. Once a route exists on both sides, repeat that route instead of pooling unlike roads. On **each** of the six final routes, record identical-camera moving screenshots, renderer, fps/frame/full-step/Jolt p99, traffic at ≥4/8 px, physical/awake body counts and a two-second contact burst. Require median **≥20 cars at 4 px** and **≥12 at 8 px**, plus ≤5 s map load. The UHD 620 comparison is a regression gate; the CTO also drives the three venues on his M1.

Keep the current near-driving-body cap until a route-specific measurement justifies changing it. Use instanced, unshadowed scenery and shared low-poly materials; target **≤150 KB added gzip** for W2 route/scenery code and assets, reporting the actual bundle change rather than silently moving the baseline. The fork, merge and guardrails must have matching render/collision edges, and far traffic must keep the existing visual/physical handoff rule.

**First probes and failure modes.** A coastline may read as recolored circuit: graybox the water edge, cliff and fork, then ask the CTO to identify it with HUD hidden before adding props. A highway bypass can create an invalid checkpoint shortcut or a vehicle-snagging merge: test the two gate chains and drive the join at 35, 60 and 85 m/s in real Jolt before traffic. Long sight lines can expose traffic pop and raise draw cost: capture matched-camera 4/8 px counts and interleave the GPU runs with the same traffic density. Added traffic may grow pileups or defeat queues: reuse W1's path-aware traffic and keep the existing physical pool/awake-body limits unless the pileup probe and CTO drive pass.
