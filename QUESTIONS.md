| 6 | developer 1 (WP5) | Design 6.10 impulse limiting (`|F|*dt <= m_wheel*|v_slip|`) applied to all tire forces would forbid launching from rest, since slip velocity is zero at standstill so the cap is zero. | PM decision 2026-09-20: apply the cap only to DISSIPATIVE forces that oppose an existing slip velocity (lateral tire force, braking, handbrake), never to engine drive force.
